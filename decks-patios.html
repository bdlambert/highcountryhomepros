Decks & Patios Built for Mountain Living

In the High Country, the deck is where the house earns its view. We build from footer to completion — engineered for snow load, sloped lots, and twenty years of sunsets — and we repair and rebuild the decks that weren't.

Built for the lot you actually have

Flat yards are rare up here. We build on slopes, over walkouts, and around rock — with footers that reach solid ground and framing sized for real mountain snow loads, not flatland spans. If your current deck bounces, leans, or scares your guests, that's fixable too.

The full outdoor room

A great mountain deck is more than boards and rails. We build the whole outdoor space: hot tub platforms with the structure to carry them, louvered privacy walls, fire-pit patios, covered porches, stairs, and lighting — designed around how you'll actually use it.

  • New decks from footer to completion
  • Deck repair, re-decking, and structural reinforcement
  • Hot tub platforms and louvered privacy enclosures
  • Covered porches and screened rooms
  • Patios, fire-pit areas, steps, and railings

Simple pricing: your materials, our labor

Decks are the cleanest example of how we like to price: we give you the materials list, you buy the materials, and you pay us for the labor. You see every dollar, and nobody marks up your lumber.

Deck questions, answered

Can you build on a steep mountain lot?

That's most of what we do. Slopes, walkouts, and rock are normal conditions up here — footers go to solid ground and the framing is engineered for the site.

Can my existing deck be saved, or does it need replacing?

Often the frame is fine and the surface is done — re-decking and reinforcement cost far less than a rebuild. We'll inspect it and tell you which side of the line it's on.

Can the deck support a hot tub?

With the right structure underneath, yes. We build hot tub platforms constantly — and we also install and service the hot tubs themselves.

How does deck pricing work?

You buy the materials from a list we provide; you pay us for the labor. Simple, transparent, and no markup on your lumber.
