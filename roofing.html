Roofing Built for High Country Weather

Mountain roofs take a beating — wind over the ridgelines, ice in January, sun and storms all summer. We repair, replace, and upgrade roofs across the High Country, and we start with a drone inspection so you get reliable answers without anyone guessing from the ground.

Start with real information, not a sales pitch

Every roofing conversation starts the same way: what shape is the roof actually in? Our drone inspections photograph every slope, ridge, and flashing detail — including the sections you can't see from a ladder — so the decision between repair and replacement is based on evidence you can look at yourself.

Repair, replace, or upgrade to metal

Sometimes a roof needs a targeted repair and five more good years. Sometimes it needs a full tear-off. And on many mountain homes, a metal upgrade is the last roof you'll ever buy — it sheds snow, shrugs off wind, and looks right on a High Country house. We'll tell you which one your roof actually calls for.

  • Asphalt shingle replacement and repair
  • Metal roofing upgrades
  • Leak diagnosis and storm-damage repair
  • Flashing, valley, and chimney detail work
  • Drone roof inspections with photo documentation

You stay in control of the budget

Like every job we do, roofing comes with straight pricing — hourly or by the job — and a materials list up front. On many projects you buy the materials and pay us for the labor. No padded quotes, no mystery line items.

Roofing questions, answered

Do you inspect the roof before quoting?

Yes — with a drone. Every slope and detail gets photographed, so the repair-or-replace decision is based on documentation you can see, not a guess from the driveway.

Is metal roofing worth it in the High Country?

Often, yes. Metal sheds snow, handles ridge-line wind, and outlasts shingles by decades — on many mountain homes it's the last roof you'll buy. We'll tell you honestly whether your house is a good candidate.

Can you just repair a leak instead of replacing the whole roof?

If the roof has life left in it, absolutely. We do targeted repairs constantly — and because we inspect first, we'll tell you when a repair is the smart money and when it's throwing good money after bad.

How does pricing work on a roof job?

Hourly or by the job, with a materials list up front. On many projects you buy the materials and pay us for the labor — you stay in control of the spend.
