The Install Crew Behind Mountain Leisure

How the partnership works

Mountain Leisure Hot Tubs has been Western North Carolina's full-service spa company since 1995 — a family-owned Sundance® Spas dealer with a showroom right in Banner Elk. Selling the right spa is their craft. Getting it onto a sloped mountain lot, onto a deck that can actually carry it, hooked up and steaming — that's ours. We perform most of Mountain Leisure's installations across the Boone, Blowing Rock, and Banner Elk area, so the handoff from showroom floor to first soak is one smooth motion instead of three phone calls.

Mountain Leisure brings the spa

Sundance® Spas hot tubs, swim spas, saunas, and Covana covers from the Banner Elk showroom at 1136 Shawneehaw Avenue — plus the water-care products and maintenance expertise of a dealer with 30 years in the business.

We bring the build

Site assessment, deck structural reinforcement, new platforms and deck construction, crane and placement coordination, electrical coordination, and startup — the same crew that builds decks and outdoor spaces across the High Country all year.

From showroom to first soak

  1. Pick your spa at Mountain Leisure. Visit the Banner Elk showroom or call (828) 649-7727 to find the right Sundance® model.
  2. We check the site. A filled hot tub weighs as much as a car. We assess your deck or pad, and spec the supports, footings, or platform it needs.
  3. We build the structure. Reinforcement for an existing deck, a new hot tub platform, or a full deck built footer-to-completion around the spa.
  4. Delivery, placement, startup. We coordinate placement and electrical, set the tub, and get it filled, balanced, and ready.

Recommended beyond the install

The relationship doesn't stop at the tub. Mountain Leisure recommends our team to its customers for deck construction and outdoor living spaces, deck supports and structural reinforcement, and full remodels. Around the spa itself, we build louvered privacy enclosures and handle ongoing hot tub service and water care — including at the top-rated vacation rentals we run ourselves.

Partnership questions, answered

I'm buying a hot tub from Mountain Leisure — who installs it?

In the High Country, usually us. We handle most Mountain Leisure installs in the Boone, Blowing Rock, and Banner Elk area — site prep, structure, placement, and startup.

Can my existing deck hold the spa?

Not always as-built — a filled tub weighs as much as a car. We assess the framing and add posts, beams, and footings where needed, or build a platform or deck section rated for the load.

Does Mountain Leisure recommend you for other projects?

Yes — remodels, deck supports, and deck construction. If you met us through your hot tub install, the same crew can build the rest of your outdoor space.
