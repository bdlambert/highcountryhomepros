STR Design & Revenue Optimization

Short-term rental operators hire us to design spaces that book. We find the high-revenue value adds — family-stay features, standout architectural design — and then our own crew builds them. One team from idea to listing photos.

Design that pays for itself

The gap between an average rental and a top performer usually isn't square footage — it's a handful of deliberate features that raise the nightly rate, win the family bookings, and give guests something to photograph. We identify those features for your specific property and market, and because we're a construction crew first, we build them too. No handoff, no markup between designer and builder.

Families book the details

Family stays are the backbone of High Country rental revenue, and kids' spaces are what win them: themed bunk rooms with murals, reading nooks under the eaves, and playful surprises like a crawl-through cabinet passage into a kids-only hideout. We've designed and built all of these in properties we operate and for clients.

Interiors that carry a listing

Great listing photos start with built features: a stone fireplace wall, a coffered wood ceiling, a marble island under brass pendants, a tiled walk-in shower. We design and build the architectural moments that make a listing stop the scroll.

Advice from operators, not theory

We design from what our own top-rated properties — a storybook castle, cabins, geodesic domes, a treepod — teach us every week about what guests book, love, and review. Your design decisions get grounded in real revenue behavior, not trends.

STR design questions, answered

What is STR design and revenue optimization?

Designing a rental so it earns more — identifying the features that raise rates and occupancy, then building them. We do both the design and the construction with one crew.

What features actually raise booking revenue?

Family-stay features (themed bunk rooms, play passages), memorable design moments guests photograph, hot tubs with views and privacy, and finishes that look premium in photos.

Why hire a builder who also operates rentals?

We see what books and what survives guests, because we run our own top-rated properties in the Blowing Rock area. Recommendations come from real guest behavior.

Do you work with remote owners?

Yes — phone, text, and email communication, with photo documentation throughout the project.
