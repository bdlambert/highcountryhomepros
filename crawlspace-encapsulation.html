Crawlspace Encapsulation, Venting & Moisture Remediation

Crawlspace encapsulation seals the space under your home — vapor barrier, sealed walls, corrected venting — so mountain moisture stops rising into your floors and framing. We pair it with the drainage work that fixes the source, not just the symptom.

Why mountain crawlspaces stay wet

High Country lots shed serious runoff toward foundations, and humid summer air condenses in cool, vented crawlspaces. The result under thousands of area homes: damp soil, wet insulation, mold on joists, and musty air that finds its way upstairs. Left alone, it becomes rot — and rot becomes structural repair.

Seal it — and fix why it's wet

Encapsulation alone treats the symptom. Our difference is doing both sides of the job: sealing and venting the crawlspace correctly underneath, and managing water outside — grading, drainage, and culverts — so your foundation stops taking the hit in the first place. One crew, both halves.

  • Ground vapor barrier and sealed foundation walls
  • Venting correction for mountain humidity
  • Moisture and mold remediation, damaged-material replacement
  • Exterior runoff, drainage, and culvert work to address the source
  • Insulation replacement for warmer floors and lower bills

Healthier house, happier guests

For vacation rentals, a musty smell is a review-killer that cleaning can't fix. We've solved crawlspace moisture for rental properties — including our own — and the difference shows up where it counts: guest reviews and repeat bookings.

Crawlspace questions, answered

What is crawlspace encapsulation?

Sealing the space under your home — vapor barrier, sealed walls, corrected venting — so ground and air moisture stop rising into your floors and framing.

Why do High Country homes have such damp crawlspaces?

Mountain runoff toward foundations plus humid air condensing in cool vented spaces keeps many area crawlspaces wet for months — feeding mold, rot, and musty air upstairs.

Do you fix the moisture source too, or just seal it?

Both — encapsulation underneath plus exterior grading, drainage, and culvert work so water never reaches the foundation.

What are the warning signs?

Musty smells, cupping hardwood, condensation on ducts, mold on joists, damp soil or standing water under the house — and for rentals, guest complaints about odor.
