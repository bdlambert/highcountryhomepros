Drywall & Painting: Walls Smooth as Butter

Twenty-plus years behind the taping knife and the brush. We hang, finish, and paint — whole houses, single rooms, and the patch jobs other crews leave behind. If the wall isn't right, we come back until it is.

The finish everyone sees

Drywall is the one trade where every mistake is visible forever — every bad seam telegraphs through paint, every rushed patch shows at sunset. Finishing is a craft, and after two decades of it we know what a wall should look like under raking mountain light.

From bare studs to final coat

Because we also frame, repair water damage, and remodel, our drywall work usually arrives as part of a bigger picture — but we take standalone jobs too. Ceiling repair after a leak, a garage that needs finishing, a whole-house repaint before listing season.

  • Drywall hanging and finishing, level to match any spec
  • Patch and texture matching that disappears into the wall
  • Water-damage ceiling and wall repair
  • Interior painting: walls, ceilings, trim, doors
  • Repaints for vacation rentals between seasons

Done right, or we come back

Our promise on this trade is simple: walls smooth as butter, or we come back until they are. Combined with hourly pricing and a materials list up front, you know exactly what you're paying for.

Drywall questions, answered

Do you do small patch jobs, or only full rooms?

Both. A doorknob hole, a ceiling stain, a whole basement — no job is too small, and patches get the same texture-matching care as new work.

Can you repair drywall after a leak or water damage?

Yes — and unlike most drywall crews, we can also fix the leak, dry the structure, and handle the whole water-damage response ourselves.

Do you paint too, or just finish the drywall?

We do both. Same crew from bare studs to final coat, so the finish and the paint are one continuous standard, not two contractors pointing at each other.

How experienced is the crew?

More than twenty years hanging, finishing, and painting — much of it on high-end mountain homes where the finish has to hold up to big windows and hard light.

Got a wall that needs to disappear?

Patches, whole rooms, or a full repaint — call and it's handled.
