Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

High Country Home Pros ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy describes what information we collect when you visit highcountryhomepros.com or contact us, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact information you provide — such as your name, phone number, and email address when you call, text, or email us about a project.
  • Project details — information about your property and the work you'd like done.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide estimates.
  • To schedule and perform work you've requested.
  • To follow up about your project by phone, text, or email.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share your information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Text messaging

If you text us or provide your number, we may reply by SMS about your inquiry or project. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out of text communication.

Data retention

We keep contact and project records only as long as needed for business, warranty, and legal purposes.
