Water Damage Remediation & Drainage Control in the High Country

Water is the number-one enemy of a mountain house. We handle the whole fight: full-service damage response — dry-out, tear-out, rebuild — plus the runoff, drainage, and culvert work that keeps it from happening again.

One crew from damage to done

Most water damage jobs get split between a mitigation company, a framer, a drywaller, and a painter — and something always falls in the gaps. We're full service: we dry it out, tear out what's ruined, and rebuild it ourselves. Framing, insulation, drywall hanging and finishing, paint, trim, and tile are all trades we do daily, so the rebuild matches the rest of your house.

Stop it at the source

In the High Country, water damage usually starts outside — a slope shedding storm runoff at your foundation, a driveway channeling water where it shouldn't, a failed or missing culvert. Our water runoff prevention work manages that flow: grading corrections, drainage paths, and culverts installed, relocated, or repurposed so water moves past your house and landscaping instead of through them.

  • Emergency dry-out and tear-out of damaged material
  • Full rebuild: framing, insulation, drywall, paint, trim, tile
  • Runoff prevention and drainage management for mountain lots
  • Culvert installation, relocation, and repurposing
  • Follow-through into crawlspace moisture control when the problem lives under the house

Protecting rentals and second homes

Many of our clients aren't on site to catch a leak early. As vacation-rental operators ourselves, we understand fast response and photo-documented work — you see what we found and what we fixed, even from three states away.

Water questions, answered

Do you handle the whole water damage job or just part of it?

The whole job — dry-out, tear-out, and rebuild with one crew, so nothing falls between contractors.

Can you stop the water problem from coming back?

Yes — grading corrections, drainage management, and culvert work move water past your house and landscaping instead of through them.

Do you install or move culverts?

Yes: installation, relocation, and repurposing for driveways and mountain drainage.

My crawlspace stays damp — is that a water problem?

Usually, and often a drainage problem first. We handle the exterior source and the crawlspace itself — see our crawlspace encapsulation page.
