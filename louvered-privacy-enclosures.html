Louvered Privacy That Opens and Closes Like Your Blinds

Mountain lots sit closer together than the views suggest — and nothing kills a hot tub soak like an audience. These aren't fixed screens. The louver slats tilt open and closed just like the blinds on your windows: open them for the view and the breeze, close them and you're completely out of sight.

Privacy you can turn up and down

This is the part most people don't expect: the louvers move. Same idea as the blinds on your living room windows — a light touch tilts the slats through their full range. Wide open, you keep the long view and the breeze coming across the deck. Fully closed, the wall is solid to the eye and nobody sees a thing. And every setting between the two is available whenever the sun, the wind, or the neighbors change your mind.

Sections operate independently, too. Open the side facing the mountains, keep the side facing the road shut — one deck, two completely different walls, adjusted in seconds.

Better than a solid fence, in every weather

A solid fence turns your deck into a box — dark, windless, and permanent. You get privacy whether you wanted it that hour or not, and you lose the view you paid for. An adjustable louvered wall gives you the same privacy on demand and hands it back when you're done. In a hard mountain wind you can even open the slats and let the gust pass straight through instead of fighting it.

Designed for your deck, not from a kit

Every enclosure we build is custom: sized to your deck, positioned against your actual sightlines, and finished to match the house. Around hot tubs we design for the soak — privacy at seated height, stars overhead, towel hooks where you want them, and the controls within reach of the water.

Part of the whole outdoor space

Privacy walls usually arrive alongside the rest of the outdoor project — a new deck, a hot tub install, a fire-pit area. One crew designs and builds it as one space, the way we do at our own rental properties.

Privacy enclosure questions, answered

Do the louvers actually open and close?

Yes — that's the whole point. They work like the blinds on your windows: tilt them open for the view and the breeze, closed for complete privacy, or anywhere in between. Sections adjust independently, so the mountain-view side can be wide open while the neighbor side stays shut.

Do louvers actually block the view in?

Closed, completely — no gaps, no sightline from the uphill house, the road, or the next deck over. Open, the view and air come right back. Privacy becomes a setting instead of a permanent condition.

Will a privacy wall make my deck feel closed in?

No, because it's only closed when you want it closed. Left open, light and air move straight through and the deck feels exactly as big as it did before.

Can you build one around an existing hot tub?

Yes — most of our enclosures go around tubs already in place. We design at soaking height, so privacy is where you sit, the stars stay overhead, and the controls are within reach of the water.

Will it survive mountain wind?

It's built to — we engineer posts and connections for High Country gusts. And when a real storm rolls through, you can open the louvers and let the wind pass through instead of pushing on a solid wall.
