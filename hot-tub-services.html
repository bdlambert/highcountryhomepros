Hot Tub Install, Repair & Water Care

A mountain hot tub is the best seat in the High Country — when the water is clear, the heater works, and the deck underneath was built for the weight. We handle all three: installation, repair, and the ongoing care that keeps it ready.

Installed right, from the ground up

A filled hot tub weighs as much as a car, and mountain sites are rarely flat. We handle the whole install: site prep or platform construction, placement, electrical coordination, and first fill and startup. Because we build decks too, "can the deck hold it?" is a question we answer with framing, not guesswork.

Repair and rescue

Heaters, pumps, controls, leaks, covers — hot tubs fail in a handful of predictable ways, usually the week your guests arrive. We diagnose and repair, and when a tub is truly done we'll tell you straight and handle the swap.

  • New installation: site prep, platforms, placement, startup
  • Repairs: heaters, pumps, controls, leaks
  • Scheduled water care and maintenance visits
  • Rental turnover service: tested, balanced, guest-ready
  • Louvered privacy enclosures built around your tub

Water care that guests can feel

We run hot tubs at our own top-rated rentals, where water quality shows up in reviews within days. That's the standard our maintenance visits hold: clear, balanced, and ready — every arrival, every soak.

Hot tub questions, answered

Can you install a hot tub on a sloped mountain lot?

Yes — site prep and platform construction are part of the job. We build the structure to carry the weight, then place, connect, and start the tub.

Do you service hot tubs at vacation rentals?

Constantly — including our own. Between-stay turnovers get the water tested, balanced, and guest-ready so reviews never mention the tub for the wrong reason.

My hot tub stopped heating — can you fix it?

Usually, yes. Heaters, pumps, controls, and leaks are the standard failures and we repair all of them. If the tub is beyond saving, we'll say so and handle the replacement.

Do you offer ongoing water care?

Yes — scheduled maintenance visits keep the water clear and balanced year-round, so the tub is always ready when you are.
